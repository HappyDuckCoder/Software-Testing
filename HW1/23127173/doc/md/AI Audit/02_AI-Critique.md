# AI Critique

Trong Requirement 1, AI ho tro tot viec chuan hoa 10 JD dai thanh mot bo Markdown/CSV co cau truc, rut gon mo ta cong viec, tach ky nang, muc luong, AI relevance va viet nhap AI Impact Analysis. Loi ich lon nhat la toc do sap xep thong tin: cac vai tro QA/QC co nhieu noi dung lap lai nhu test planning, automation, defect tracking, CI/CD, API testing va reporting, nen AI giup gom nhom nhanh hon.

Tuy nhien, AI co rui ro suy luan qua muc neu JD khong noi ro mot truong nao do. Vi du, khi salary chi ghi "competitive" hoac khong co so cu the, AI khong duoc phep tu tao con so. Khi mot posting chi neu automation ma khong neu AI/LLM, AI cung khong nen gan nhan "AI skill" chi vi vai tro co lien quan cong nghe. Diem can canh giac nua la AI co the tom tat qua ngan lam mat cac yeu cau quan trong nhu prompt regression, AI-agent safety, model degradation, hoac data quality tools.

Bai hoc la nen dung AI nhu cong cu bien tap va kiem tra tinh day du, khong dung nhu nguon su that. Sinh vien phai giu link, screenshot, JD goc va tu xac minh cac truong nhay cam nhu ngay dang, salary, company, location. Voi QA/QC, cach lam dung la tach ro du lieu goc, tom tat cua AI va phan nhan xet ca nhan.
