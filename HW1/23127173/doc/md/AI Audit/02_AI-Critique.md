# AI Critique

Trong Requirement 1, AI ho tro tot viec chuan hoa 10 JD dai thanh mot bo Markdown/CSV co cau truc, rut gon mo ta cong viec, tach ky nang, muc luong, AI relevance va viet nhap AI Impact Analysis. Loi ich lon nhat la toc do sap xep thong tin: cac vai tro QA/QC co nhieu noi dung lap lai nhu test planning, automation, defect tracking, CI/CD, API testing va reporting, nen AI giup gom nhom nhanh hon.

Tuy nhien, AI co rui ro suy luan qua muc neu JD khong noi ro mot truong nao do. Vi du, khi salary chi ghi "competitive" hoac khong co so cu the, AI khong duoc phep tu tao con so. Khi mot posting chi neu automation ma khong neu AI/LLM, AI cung khong nen gan nhan "AI skill" chi vi vai tro co lien quan cong nghe. Diem can canh giac nua la AI co the tom tat qua ngan lam mat cac yeu cau quan trong nhu prompt regression, AI-agent safety, model degradation, hoac data quality tools.

Voi Requirement 3, AI ho tro nhanh trong viec bien anh remote dieu hoa thanh danh sach chuc nang can test: Power, Mode, Speed, Turbo, Swing, iSAVE, Baby Care, Menu/OK va nhiet do. AI cung huu ich khi ep moi test case co Objective, Input, Steps, Expected, Actual va Verdict. Tuy nhien, AI that bai neu bi dung nhu nguon ket qua: no khong the biet hang/model/serial that, khong the xac nhan dieu hoa co nhan tin hieu IR, va khong the ket luan defect neu sinh vien chua chay tren thiet bi. Thien kien cua AI la hay tao cac test "dep" theo chuc nang nut bam, bo qua trang thai tien dieu kien va tac dong che do phu. Ba edge case sinh vien tu tim ra cho thay diem nay ro hon: nut Turbo/Mode/Speed co the bat may khi dieu hoa dang tat, chuyen Cool sang Dry lam quat tu ve muc yeu nhat, va Baby Care khoa cac cau hinh nhu nhiet do/huong gio.

Bai hoc la nen dung AI nhu cong cu bien tap, kiem tra tinh day du, va tao danh sach y tuong ban dau, khong dung nhu nguon su that. Sinh vien phai giu link, screenshot, JD goc, anh/video that, va tu xac minh cac truong nhay cam nhu ngay dang, salary, company, location, model, serial, actual result va defect. Voi QA/QC, cach lam dung la tach ro du lieu goc, tom tat cua AI, ket qua quan sat that va phan nhan xet ca nhan.
