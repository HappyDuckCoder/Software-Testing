## Job Description

The Design Verification Engineer ensures the functionality and correctness of complex hardware or software designs. The role works closely with design engineers to verify that the final product meets requirements and specifications.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop verification test plans based on design specifications.
- Execute tests using simulation tools, emulation platforms, or FPGA prototypes.
- Debug failures, analyze waveforms, and resolve issues with design teams.
- Analyze functional, code, and assertion coverage.
- Apply formal verification and assertion-based techniques.
- Run regression tests and maintain regression suites.
- Document test plans, results, and verification methodologies.
- Collaborate with design, architecture, and software teams.

## Required Skills

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related field.
- Verilog or SystemVerilog.
- UVM or other verification methodologies.
- Perl, Python, or TCL scripting.
- PCIe, USB, DDR, or similar industry-standard interfaces.
- CDNS EDA tools for design verification.

## Salary / Benefits

Competitive salary range up to USD 5,000 is mentioned.

## AI / Automation Relevance

No explicit AI skill is required, but regression automation and verification tooling are central to the role.

## AI Impact Analysis

AI can help summarize logs, propose regression scenarios, and identify coverage gaps, but hardware/design verification still requires rigorous evidence, domain knowledge, and formal reasoning.
