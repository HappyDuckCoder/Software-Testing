## Job Description

OptiSigns is looking for a QA - Software / Device Manager to ensure the quality and reliability of both software applications and hardware/device solutions. The role oversees QA processes, defines quality metrics, manages the QA team, and collaborates with development and product teams to achieve quality excellence.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and implement QA strategies for software applications and hardware devices.
- Oversee QA team activities and ensure effective testing processes.
- Manage the testing lifecycle, including test case design, execution, and defect tracking.
- Collaborate with development teams to resolve defects and quality issues.
- Establish quality metrics and reporting for product performance and improvement areas.
- Ensure compliance with QA industry standards and best practices.
- Train and mentor QA team members.

## Required Skills

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- 5+ years of software and hardware QA experience.
- Ability to manage 4-5 junior QA engineers.
- Experience leading QA teams and managing testing processes.
- Strong knowledge of testing methodologies, tools, and QA best practices.
- Automation testing tools/frameworks are a plus.
- Analytical, problem-solving, communication, and cross-team collaboration skills.

## Salary / Benefits

Competitive pay; exact salary is not disclosed. Benefits include U.S. office travel opportunity, latest MacBook Pro, company trip, paid time off, and insurance.

## AI / Automation Relevance

Automation testing tools/frameworks are mentioned as a plus. No explicit AI/LLM requirement is listed.

## AI Impact Analysis

AI can help this role summarize defect trends, generate QA metrics, and identify high-risk areas across software and device releases. Human QA leadership is still required for team mentoring, quality strategy, and release decisions.
