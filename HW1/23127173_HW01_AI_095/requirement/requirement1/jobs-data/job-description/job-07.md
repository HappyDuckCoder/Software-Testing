## Job Description

SUN.STUDIO is hiring a hands-on Senior Manual QA to raise game product quality and ensure production releases meet quality standards. The company develops web and mobile games, especially hyper-casual, casual, and puzzle games.

## Key Responsibilities

- Ensure production releases do not contain critical bugs.
- Design test plans for puzzle, casual, and hyper-casual games on Google Play.
- Test ads, data analytics, localization, ANR, UI, UX, device compatibility, FPS, and loading time.
- Maintain release checklists, acceptance criteria, and exploratory cases.
- Execute test runs and produce test reports based on test plans.
- Write and update test cases using templates and defined processes.
- Report bugs, verify fixes, manage issue lifecycle, and improve QA processes.
- Analyze complex issues and work with BOD, Marketing, and Tech teams.
- Track task/ticket status and project quality metrics.

## Required Skills

- Experience testing at least two game projects with 1M+ downloads or successful user/revenue outcomes.
- At least 1 year in a Senior Game Tester role.
- Ownership in long-term game projects.
- Strong experience creating quality documents, checklists, test suites, and test templates.
- Communication, problem solving, self-learning, and continuous improvement.
- IT, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related degree.
- ISTQB, ASTQB, ISO, or similar testing certificate is preferred.

## Salary / Benefits

Exact salary is not disclosed. Benefits include insurance, PVI healthcare, 13th-month salary, performance bonus, seniority bonus, holiday bonus, training, lunch allowance, pantry, game area, happy hour, company events, and team building.

## AI / Automation Relevance

The posting states that AI tools may support recruitment activities. The QA role itself is manual game testing and does not list AI/LLM as a required testing skill.

## AI Impact Analysis

AI can help generate checklist ideas and exploratory test scenarios, but game QA still requires human playtesting, UX judgment, device compatibility checks, and careful bug verification.
